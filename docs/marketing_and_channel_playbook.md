# SwasthAI Multi-Channel Marketing & Outreach Playbook

This document defines the official distribution rules and ready-to-use templates for SwasthAI across **Cold Email Outreach**, **Website Positioning**, and **LinkedIn Content**.

---

## 1. 📧 Cold Email Follow-Up Strategy

### The Core Rule:
> **Never send the flyer in the initial cold email.**  
> Initial cold outreach must always be ultra-short (90–140 words), personalized, plain text, and focused on starting a conversation.  
> **Only when a doctor or clinic manager replies with interest, send the flyer + interactive demo link.**

## 2. 🌐 Website Integration Strategy (Vercel)

### The Core Rule:
> **Do not put the entire flyer on the homepage.**  
> The homepage should explain SwasthAI with interactive UI components, clear benefits, testimonials, and live product demos.  
> Keep the flyer as a downloadable one-pager (PDF/Image) on the **Demo** and **Contact** pages for doctors who want to print or share it with partners.

### Live Website Integration:
- **Interactive Web Flyer:** `https://swasthai-three.vercel.app/flyer.html` (includes 1-click **"Print / Save as PDF"** at 300 DPI).
- **Direct Image Download:** `https://swasthai-three.vercel.app/img/flyer.jpg`.
- **Download Points:** Added clean download cards on both `/demo` and `/contact` pages.

## 3. 💼 LinkedIn Content Strategy & Post Templates

### The Core Rule:
> **Don't make the flyer your everyday LinkedIn post.**  
> Regular LinkedIn posts should feature real product screenshots, UI clips, or clinical insights.  
> Use the flyer **strategically and occasionally** for:
> 1. Launch / Founder Announcements
> 2. "We're starting clinic pilots" invitations
> 3. Major product updates
> 4. Event / Conference announcements
