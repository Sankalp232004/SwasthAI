## 4. 💼 LinkedIn Content Strategy

### The Core Rule:
> **Don't repeatedly post the entire pitch deck on LinkedIn.**  
> Instead, share:
> 1. Individual ideas from specific slides
> 2. Real product screenshots & UI walkthroughs
> 3. Clinic workflow observations & waiting room friction points
> 4. Pilot results and doctor feedback once live
>
> **If someone asks about SwasthAI in comments or DMs, send them the website and pitch deck directly.**

### Ready-to-Post LinkedIn Templates:

#### Post 1: The 75-Minute Math (From Slide 2)
```text
Here is a quiet operational leak in Indian outpatient clinics:

The first 90 seconds of every consultation is spent on basic symptom extraction that could have happened at reception.

50 patients × 90 seconds = 75 minutes of lost clinical time every single day.

Paper registers capture arrival timestamps, but they have zero clinical context. A febrile child and a routine follow-up wait in the exact same line.

We built SwasthAI so patients complete a structured 60-second digital intake at reception (zero app downloads). When the doctor opens the door, the chief complaint and urgency level are already on screen.

75 minutes saved. Urgent walk-ins spotted immediately.

DM me if you'd like to see our 7-slide clinic deck or test the live demo.

#ClinicManagement #DigitalHealth #HealthcareOperations #SwasthAI
```

#### Post 2: "Doctor-in-Control" Safety Principle (From Slide 4 & 6)
```text
The #1 concern doctors have when they hear "AI queue system":

"Is an algorithm trying to make clinical decisions for me?"

Our answer is always absolute: No.

SwasthAI is an operational triage assistant, NOT a diagnostic engine.
• It surfaces acute walk-ins (chest pain, high fevers, severe trauma) to the top of the queue.
• The doctor retains 100% override authority to reorder, bump, or group patients with one click.
• Every action is logged with a timestamp for a full audit trail.

Technology should give doctors clarity, not take away clinical autonomy.
```
