## 🎙️ Slide-by-Slide 5-Minute Pitch Script

### Slide 1: Title & Core Value Proposition
> *"Good morning, Dr. [Name]. Thank you for taking 5 minutes to connect.*  
> *I built SwasthAI around one simple, universal premise: in a busy Indian outpatient clinic, the most acute patient should never be waiting in the dark.*  
> *SwasthAI is a zero-friction, QR-based patient intake and clinical queue prioritization system that helps your practice organize walk-ins objectively in under 60 seconds."*

---

### Slide 2: The First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Bottleneck
> *"Every doctor we’ve spoken with faces the exact same daily reality in their reception lobby:*  
> *Patients are seen strictly in the order they walked in through the door.*  
> *That means a child with 103.5°F fever or an acute joint trauma is often sitting behind three routine follow-ups and medication refills.*  
> *Your receptionist faces angry arguments, and you as the doctor spend the first 3 minutes of every 8-minute consult just extracting basic symptoms from scratch."*

---

### Slide 3: The 3-Step SwasthAI Workflow
> *"Here is how SwasthAI fixes this without requiring any new hardware or software downloads:*  
> 1. **Step 1 (Arrival):** The patient scans a desk QR code on their own smartphone as soon as they walk in. No app download needed.  
> 2. **Step 2 (60-Sec Intake):** They answer 4–5 structured questions in plain English or regional languages — chief complaint, duration, and red-flag symptoms.  
> 3. **Step 3 (Priority Queue):** SwasthAI evaluates clinical parameters and surfaces a recommended priority queue on your consultation console with instant pre-consult summaries."*

---

### Slide 4: Doctor Console & 100% Doctor Control
> *"I want to emphasize our core clinical boundary: **SwasthAI is NOT a diagnostic engine. It does NOT prescribe or treat.***  
> *It is an operational triage assistant.  
> As the doctor, you retain **100% final override control**. You can reorder, bump, or group patients with a single click at any time.  
> What you gain is clarity: before you open the door, you already know the chief complaint and urgency level of the next patient."*

---

### Slide 5: Specialty-Specific Adaptations
> *"We configure specific question trees for your specialty in under 2 hours:*  
> • **Pediatrics:** Spotting high fever, respiratory distress, and lethargy immediately.  
> • **Orthopedics:** Separating acute fractures and dislocations from routine arthritis checkups.  
> • **ENT / Derma:** Highlighting acute bleeding or severe pain vs routine consultations.  
> • **General Medicine:** Surfacing vitals anomalies and acute chest/abdominal distress."*

---

### Slide 6: Measurable Clinic ROI
> *"When clinics run SwasthAI:*  
> • **Save 2–3 Minutes per Consult:** You skip the repetitive administrative intake and jump straight into clinical examination.  
> • **90% Drop in Lobby Arguments:** Patients see their position on a transparent screen and understand why urgent cases are called first.  
> • **Offline-First Resilience:** If clinic Wi-Fi drops, the local dashboard continues running smoothly."*

---

> *We offer a **Free 2-Day Live Trial** during your busiest morning and evening sessions.*  
> • ₹0 Setup Fee.

> • 10-minute reception onboarding.  
> If you have your smartphone ready, you can scan the QR code on the screen right now to experience the live intake app.

## 🛡️ Clinical Objection Handling Guide

| Objection | Doctor's Concern | Recommended Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"What if elderly patients don't have a smartphone?"** | Accessibility / Adoption | *"Great question. The receptionist can enter their details in 20 seconds on the front-desk tablet, or an accompanying family member can scan the QR code. Nobody is excluded."* |
| **"Will this slow down my reception desk?"** | Workflow friction | *"It actually speeds up reception by 70%. Instead of filling paper registers and handwriting receipts, patients self-serve on their phones while seated in the waiting room."* |
| **"Is AI trying to replace my medical decision?"** | Autonomy & Liability | *"Absolutely not. SwasthAI is strictly an operational triage filter. It assists with queue sorting, but the doctor always makes 100% of clinical decisions and can override the queue anytime."* |
| **"What happens if our clinic internet goes down?"** | Reliability | *"SwasthAI is built offline-first. It caches patient submissions locally on your clinic network and synchronizes automatically when connection resumes."* |
